Class Features

Hunter When no ally is closer to an enemy than you are, you get +1 to attack rolls against that enemy. You gain this bonus against all enemies who meet these criteria.

Wild Grace Ignore the armor check penalty from hide armor.

Sharp Edge Once per round, do +1d6 damage on a hit. Level 11 +2d6 instead. Level 21 +3d6 instead.

Brew Poison After each long rest, you may brew one poison for each point of Stamina you spend. Each poison must be of your level or lower. You cannot brew the same variety of poison twice in the same day. You can give poisons to allies. If any poison is not used by the end of your next long rest, it becomes ineffective.
