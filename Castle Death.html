Castle Death

Dungeon World starter by Mark Tygart. Adapted for Orcus by Chris Sakkas. Released under a Creative Commons 4.0 license. Recommended for heroes of levels 1 through 3.

Questions

  • What brings you here, to Castle Death?
  • Have you been in this adventuring business for long?
  • What stories have you heard about the cruel tyrant said to have once ruled this place?
  • What do the locals say they see or hear at night when the battlements are observed from a safe distance?
  • What rumors of wealth are associated with the keep?
  • Do you believe in the prophecy of the return of a rightful lord?

Impressions

  • A stark silhouette against a stormy sky
  • Ancient stonework, vine-cloaked walls
  • A flash of lightning, deafening thunder
  • Rain and wind across the crumbling battlements
  • The gaping maw of the front gate
  • A raised portcullis, like a line of iron teeth
  • The stench of goblin
  • The flickering light of torches, here and there
  • A high-ceiling passage, dense with thick webs
  • Rich and soiled furnishings, looted from elsewhere
  • The distant sound of guttural laughter

Creeping Doom

If the denizens of Castle Death have reason to be suspicious ...

After a short rest, the GM chooses one.

  • Bongaz prepares a Potion of Healing from his treasure trove.
  • Mordrivaug dons the Ring of Shadows and slips out of his chambers to investigate.
  • The torches in one room burn low, threatening to plunge it into darkness mid-encounter.
  • Night approaches. Skeletal wretches gain +2 Speed.
  • The goblins light bonfires for their next meal. Smoke spreads.

After a long rest, the GM chooses one.

  • Weak-hearted goblins are rooted out. No option for goblin recruits.
  • Hobgoblin soldiers arrive.
  • Mordrivaug breaks the Lightblade in two. It will need repairs.
  • Some treasure is sent away as tribute.
  • The Book of Wonders is cleverly hidden.

Places

Hidden Sanctuary The first time you take a short rest in the Hidden Sanctuary, one hero makes a Dungeoneering check (DC 15). On a success, choose three. On a failure, choose one.

  • You recover as if you had taken a long rest
  • You each collect an additional 1d6 days’ worth of rations
  • You experience a vision (ask the GM what you see)
  • You do not collapse into mystical sleep

Throne Room When you enter the Throne Room for the first time, each make Stealth and Perception checks (DC 14).

If most succeed on Perception, they notice the Pit Traps.

If most succeed on Stealth, Bongaz is asleep.

Discoveries

☐ Goblin Recruit A goblin lackey joins the party. Later heroes can start with the goblin ancestry.

☐ Grave Goods Salamander Amulet, Old Map, gold cartouche (250 gold)

Salamander Amulet An arrowhead-shaped amulet of polished black onyx on a square-linked chain.

Old Map A 3’x3’ piece of vellum, soft as silk from a lifetime of folding and unfolding. Fingerprints, scratch marks, and unidentifiable stains (blood? tea?) obscure a detailed architectural schematic in faded brown ink. A floorplan of Castle Death. You may discern the location of the Hidden Sanctuary, and one or more of the Architect’s Pit Traps.

☐ Goblin Bank Codex of Wonders, 60 gold, 300 silver

Codex of Wonders A thick tome with a latching cover, bound in canvas that appears gray, but upon close examination swirls with living color.

☐ Bongaz's Stash Potion of Healing, 70 gold, 100 silver, ruby earring (100 gold)

☐ Mordrivaug's Horde The Lightblade, Ring of Shadows, Deed to Castle Death, 2 Potions of Healing, 260 gold, ruby earring (100 gold)

The Lightblade A short sword glowing white, as easy to wield as a willow switch.

Ring of Shadows A simple band of smoky quartz.

Deed to Castle Death A sheaf of yellowed pages, tied up with string. When you decipher the ridiculous legal language of the Deed, you learn that a member of your party is the true and rightful heir to Castle Death! Ask the GM who it is.

Level 1, 50 XP
Each square is either black or white. On odd rounds, spikes attack creatures standing on black squares. On even rounds, spikes attack creatures standing on white squares. +4 vs Reflex; 1d10+3 damage.

♕︎ Marble Queen ● Cover

Medium. AC/Ref 5, Fort 10, HP 20
DC 12 Athletics to rotate. While the queen faces the chequerboard, pattern swaps: on odd rounds, spikes attack white. On even, spikes attack black.

Level 1, 50 XP

+4 vs Reflex (all creatures standing in the area). On a hit, the target falls 10 feet into the pit (1d10 falling damage).

On a success, the GM will answer two of the questions correctly. On a failure, the GM will answer two of the questions incorrectly.
